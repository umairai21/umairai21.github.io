About

I'm a Data Analyst and aspiring Data Scientist based in Dubai, currently doing my MSc in AI and Data Science at Middlesex University Dubai. My path here started in software engineering, which turned out to be a pretty good foundation, I spent time building automations, working with ERP systems, and writing SQL to solve real operational problems. Somewhere along the way I realized the part I actually cared about wasn't just building the system, it was understanding what the data coming out of it was trying to tell people. That's what pulled me toward analytics.

These days my work sits across Python, SQL, and Power BI: cleaning up messy data, digging into it to find what's actually going on, and turning that into dashboards and insights people can act on. There's something satisfying about untangling something genuinely complicated.

I'm currently open to new opportunities in data analytics, including internships, and always happy to talk data.
