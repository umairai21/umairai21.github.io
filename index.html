About

I'm currently based in Dubai, completing an MSc in AI and Data Science at Middlesex University Dubai. My core interests are data analytics, data science, and machine learning.

I got into data through software engineering. I spent time building automations, working with ERP systems, and writing SQL to solve real operational problems. Along the way I realised the part I cared about most was understanding what the data from those systems was telling people. Today my work spans the full data lifecycle: cleaning and exploring data with Python and SQL, building dashboards in Power BI, and, through my MSc, applying statistical modelling and machine learning to build, evaluate, and interpret predictive models. Whether the result is a dashboard or a model, the goal is the same: turning complex data into something people can act on.

That engineering foundation is still a big part of how I work. I build software and AI-driven automations, and integrate AI tools into my day-to-day workflows to remove repetitive work and get to insights faster.
